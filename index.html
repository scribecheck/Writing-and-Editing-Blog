Hi, I'm Colin. I'm an American living and working in Japan. I have three cats, a lot of plants, and far too many crafting projects I'm in the middle of finishing.

I've been an English teacher for 12 years, and a freelance writer and editor for 7 years alongside that. Throughout my career of marketing myself, I've struggled with the same questions as other freelancers and small business owners:

How do I stand out?

Why am I getting engagement but no calls?

How do I help the right people find me?

Cliche as it sounds, we're much better at seeing what makes other people special than seeing it in ourselves. Every small business has unique selling points that are exactly what real people are looking for.

My job is to develop and write marketing content that shows people why your business meets their needs, fixes their problems, and delivers what they want. I'm good at research, strategic analyzing, and explaining. And I love helping other business owners build their own success.

If you'd like to have a casual chat to learn more, you can book a (free!) 30 minute call through Google Calendar. Or, shoot an email to my inbox at cmsavage@scribecheck.net.
